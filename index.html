About me

I'm a second-year Data Science student at MPSTME, NMIMS Mumbai. I finished school at Wynberg Allen in Mussoorie before moving to the city to study.

I like projects where data meets a real problem: keeping patient records private on a device, finding out what actually decides a cricket match, or turning a hundred thousand orders into a story a business could act on. I learn fastest by building something, deploying it, and seeing where it breaks.

  • Education: B.Tech in Data Science, NMIMS (CGPA 7.92), 2024 to 2028. Class XII (ICSE), 91.2%.

  • Exploring: RAG, AI agents, LangGraph and LLM applications
